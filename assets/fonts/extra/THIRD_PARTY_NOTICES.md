# Third-party notices

jubarte does not embed these fonts. `scripts/install.sh` (`install.ps1` on
Windows) copies them into jubarte's font folder, and the release archives
carry them in `fonts/`, so a document that names them renders as Word draws
it. They are unmodified, each under its own licence, whose text is beside
them and travels with them.
